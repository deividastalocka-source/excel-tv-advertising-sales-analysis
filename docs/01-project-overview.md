# Project Overview

## Introduction

This project investigated whether there is a linear relationship between expenditure on TV advertising and product sales.

The analysis was completed as a university group project using Microsoft Excel. Statistical techniques were applied to a dataset containing 200 observations of TV advertising expenditure and corresponding product sales.

The project covered:

- Scatterplot analysis
- Linear regression
- Correlation analysis
- Coefficient of determination
- Residual analysis
- Confidence intervals
- Hypothesis testing

## Research Question

The main question investigated was:

> **Is there a linear relationship between the funds allocated to TV advertising and the number of sales of the company's products?**

TV advertising expenditure was treated as the **explanatory variable** because it represents the factor that may influence sales.

Product sales were treated as the **response variable** because sales may change depending on the amount invested in advertising.

## Dataset

The dataset contained **200 observations** of:

- TV advertising expenditure
- Product sales

TV advertising expenditure was measured in hundreds of pounds while sales were measured in thousands.
